## Deliberately not used

Official Enagic pages describe the K8 as an "antioxidant machine" and discuss
ORP, "active oxygen", hydrogen and a Japanese medical-device certification
(227AGBZX00107000, Japan only). None of this appears on the site: the brief
forbids turning technical properties into health claims, and the Japanese
certification does not apply in the Philippines.

**Caveat on the product image.** It is a studio image, not a 3D model. Its
display panel matches the manual's home screen (EN9) — pH 7.0 / 9.5, 6.0 / 9.0,
2.5 and Setting — except the last tile, which reads "pH6.5" where the real
K8 shows pH 8.5; that error is in the client's reference photograph itself.
The close-up chapter is limited to 1.38× (wide) / 1.5× (compact) and carries
a caption that on-screen details may differ. **Replace it with an official
Enagic photograph — or an accurate 3D model (docs/3d-model-upgrade.md) —
when one is available.**

**Caveat on the angled image.** It was generated by AI, not photographed.
Its display tiles (pH 7.0, 8.5, 9.0, 9.5, 2.5, 11.5) don't match the manual's
home screen (no Beauty 6.0; no 11.5 setting — Strong Kangen is about 11.0);
it carried a gold "WQA" seal and text claiming NSF/ANSI 42, 53, 58 and 372
certification and a patent number, none of which is verified here (the site
makes no certification claim from it) — the seal, its C / USA marks and that
text were all painted out on 2026-09-16 at the owner's request; and it shows
the secondary hose
rising to an upright nozzle, where the manual (EN10) says to keep that pipe
lower than the machine. The display close-up uses the front image only.
Details: `docs/3d-model-upgrade.md` → "Accuracy of the current angled image".
**Replace it with an authentic angled photograph when one is available.**
